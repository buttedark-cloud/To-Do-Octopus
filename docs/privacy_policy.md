# Privacy Policy — To-Do Octopus

Last updated: [ДАТА]

To-Do Octopus ("the app") is a to-do list, notes and calendar app for Android.
This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

## Data we collect
- **Account data:** email address, display name and profile photo (when you sign in with email or Google).
- **Content you create:** tasks, categories, notes, reminder dates, settings, and pictures you attach to notes or use as an avatar.

## How we use it
The data is used only to run the app: to sign you in, to store your tasks and notes, and to keep them in sync between your devices. We do not use it for advertising and do not sell or share it with third parties.

## Where data is stored
- Account data and your tasks, notes and settings are stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore).
- Pictures are stored with Supabase (Storage).
All data is sent over encrypted connections. A copy of your tasks and notes is also kept on your device.

## Reminders
Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. The app asks for notification permission and, optionally, to run without battery restrictions so reminders arrive on time.

## Deleting your data
You can delete your account and all its data at any time in the app: Profile → Settings → Account → "Delete account". See also: [ССЫЛКА НА СТРАНИЦУ delete-account]

## Children
The app is not directed to children under 13.
